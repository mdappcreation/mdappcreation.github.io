WMPR App Manager Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

WMPR App Manager is developed by MD App Creation. This policy explains how the app and the third-party services it uses process information.

1. Information Processed on Your Device

When you scan, WMPR reads the inventory of user-installed, pre-installed, and system apps on your device. Depending on Android availability, this may include app name, package identifier, version, icon, package size, installation and update dates, installation source, launcher-icon availability, declared permissions and their grant status, and special access such as Accessibility Service, Device Administrator, display over other apps, and installation of unknown apps.

2. How the Information Is Used

WMPR uses this information to list, categorize, filter, sort, and explain installed apps, show app details, and help you open Android's uninstall or permission settings.

WMPR does not read the content of messages, contacts, call logs, location, photos, files, camera images, or microphone recordings. Categories and access indicators are informational and are not a malware verdict or a guarantee that an app is safe or harmful.

3. Local Processing, Storage, and Sharing

Installed-app information and scan results are processed and stored on your device. They are not uploaded to MD App Creation servers, sold, or shared with Google AdMob or another third party for advertising or analytics.

Android cloud backup is disabled for WMPR. Clearing app data or uninstalling WMPR removes locally stored WMPR data.

4. Permission Usage

  • QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES: Used only to discover, display, and organize installed apps.
  • REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES: Opens Android's confirmation screen when you choose to uninstall an app. WMPR cannot remove an app without the Android confirmation flow.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS and RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Support optional reminders.
  • INTERNET: Used for Google AdMob, Google Play Billing, consent and privacy services, and links you choose to open.

5. Advertising and Privacy Choices

The free version uses Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Depending on device settings, region, consent choices, and ad availability, Google may process IP-derived approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver, measure, and protect ads. Installed-app inventory is not provided to AdMob.

WMPR uses Google's User Messaging Platform where required to request consent and provide privacy choices. You can manage available privacy choices in the app, advertising settings in Android, and applicable Google choices through My Ad Center.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

6. Purchases

Google Play Billing processes purchases. WMPR receives product and purchase-status information but does not receive your full payment-card details.

An optional, non-renewing one-time purchase permanently removes ads for the purchasing Google Play account. Eligible purchases can be restored. Users with a valid legacy subscription remain ad-free until the end of their paid entitlement.

7. Children's Privacy

WMPR is not directed to children under 13, and MD App Creation does not knowingly request personal information from children through the app. Advertising and consent behavior are also subject to applicable Google policies and regional requirements.

8. Security and Changes

Installed-app analysis remains on the device. Data transmitted by supported Google services is encrypted in transit. No electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the app, third-party services, or legal requirements. The revised date will be shown on this page.
